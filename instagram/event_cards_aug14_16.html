🛍 Annual Garage Sale · Free
Maywood Park Garage Sale
The annual Maywood Park Garage Sale turns this small, tucked-away city-within-a-city into one big community swap meet. Maywood Park is one of the few remaining truly independent cities inside the Portland metro area — easy to miss if you don't know it's there. Arrive early for the best finds. Free to browse all day Friday and Saturday.
10100 NE Prescott St, Maywood Park · Fri–Sat Aug 14–15 · Free

🏎 Free · Mt. Tabor Park
Adult Soapbox Derby
One of Portland's most beloved summer spectacles — homemade, gravity-powered vehicles of wildly varying quality (and structural integrity) race down the slopes of Mt. Tabor Park. Costumes, creative engineering, and chaotic finishes are the norm. Free to watch all day from 10 AM to 4 PM. Bring snacks and a good seat-spot.
Mt. Tabor Park · Sat Aug 15 · 10 AM–4 PM · Free · @pdxsoapboxderby
